PokéBastion TD — Pokémon Tower Defense

An open-map, real-time roguelike Pokémon tower defense game in your browser, inspired by The Last Spell, Arknights, and Path to Nowhere.

Play Now View on GitHub

What Makes PokéBastion TD Different

  • Open-Map Dynamic Pathfinding: Wild Pokémon route across a 28×18 battlefield toward your central Pokémon Center. Build walls and traps to reshape their path in real time, with live dashed path previews during prep.
  • 152 Playable Gen 1 Pokémon: Draft a starting squad within a 10-point budget, recruit more between waves, evolve units, and unlock 3 distinct specialization branches per species.
  • Real-Time Tactical Repositioning: Pokémon auto-attack in range while you spend shared Action Points (AP) to reposition units mid-fight and trigger manual or auto-cast Ultimates.
  • 9 Defensive Structures & Traps: Place Barricades, Stone Walls, Thorn Walls, Flame Braziers, Berry Bushes, Tempo Shrines, Honey Traps, Lightning Rods, and Bomb Barrels.
  • 24 Held Items & Roguelike Relics: Equip up to 3 held items per Pokémon, draft team-wide class upgrades, and collect global artifacts across 20–50 wave runs.
  • Meta-Progression: Earn Species Shards, Universal Shards, Eggs, and Achievements to unlock Pokémon in the Pokédex and upgrade their Potential (★0–★5).

How to Play

  1. Draft Your Squad: Pick a map and difficulty (Easy to Expert), then assemble your starting team within the 10-point draft budget.
  2. Prep Phase: Place, move, repair, or sell structures to funnel enemies into kill zones. Reposition Pokémon freely (0 AP) and equip held items before starting the wave.
  3. Combat Phase: Spend 1 AP per move to reposition around threats (ground, burrowing, flying, and wall-breaker enemies) and trigger Ultimates. Press Esc or right-click to cancel selection or aiming.
  4. Rewards & Shop: Draft 1 of 3 roguelike rewards after waves and visit the shop every 5th wave.

Alpha Status

PokéBastion TD is currently in active Alpha development. Gameplay balance, features, and local save data formats may change between updates. Feedback and bug reports are welcome on GitHub.
